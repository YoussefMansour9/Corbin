# **App Name**: Corbin Staffing

## Core Features:

- Homepage with 'How It Works' Section: Visually engaging section detailing the three-step process: Select, Interview, and Hire, with clear descriptions for each.
- Comprehensive Services Overview: Dedicated section showcasing all services: Administrative Support, Customer Support, Sales & Marketing, Accounting & Finance, and Technical Support. Each service will have a clear description.
- Dynamic 'About Us' Section: Interactive section highlighting Corbin Staffing's mission, values, and unique selling points, such as a flexible and tailored approach to sourcing professionals.
- Team Showcase: Visually appealing section presenting team members with photos and brief bios.
- Location Showcase: An attractive section presenting office locations through embedded videos, giving users a virtual tour experience.
- EmailJS Integration for Form Submission: Seamless integration of EmailJS service for form submissions, ensuring reliable and efficient delivery of inquiries.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A vibrant blue (#29ABE2), inspired by concepts of trust and professionalism in staffing.
- Background color: A light blue (#E1F5FE), a desaturated tint of the primary blue, providing a clean and modern backdrop.
- Accent color: A dynamic purple (#9C27B0) analogous to the primary, suggesting a modern approach and high performance.
- Font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for a modern, clean and professional look in both headlines and body.
- Modern, minimalist icons representing each service and feature.
- Clean and intuitive layout with clear sections for easy navigation.
- Modern, interactive animations for a visually engaging experience.
